SmartScore compact piano bank
=============================

The piano samples in piano.pbank are adapted from:

Salamander Grand Piano V3
Author: Alexander Holm
Instrument: Yamaha C5 grand piano
Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Modifications made for SmartScore:
- selected three velocity layers from the original library;
- selected root notes sampled in minor thirds;
- downmixed to mono and resampled to 24 kHz;
- applied a modest fixed gain for the device speaker output;
- trimmed and limited each sample to a compact attack/sustain segment;
- added a crossfaded loop tail;
- packed signed 16-bit PCM into the SmartScore piano-bank container.

The original author does not endorse SmartScore or this adaptation.
